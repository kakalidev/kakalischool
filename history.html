History

Founded in 1975, Kakali Nursery Kindergarten and primary school began as a modest educational institution with a vision to provide quality education to the community. Starting with just a handful of students and dedicated teachers, the school quickly gained a reputation for academic excellence and strong values. Throughout the 1980s, the school expanded its facilities, introducing new classrooms, a library, and sports grounds to cater to its growing student population. The 1990s marked a period of modernization, with the integration of technology in teaching and the introduction of extracurricular programs aimed at holistic development. By the year 2005, Kakali had expanded to Kakali Vidyamandir high school, fostering generations of successful alumni. The school celebrated its milestones with the support of an ever-strong alumni network and a forward-thinking administration. Now, in its 50th year,Kakali continues to uphold its tradition of excellence while embracing innovation. Its golden jubilee marks a celebration of its rich history, dedicated educators, and the thousands of lives it has shaped over the decades.
